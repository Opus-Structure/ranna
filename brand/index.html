Ranna brand guide

Mark, wordmark, colour, type and spacing for Ranna, the QR-ordering platform that rings your phone when the order is ready. Every file here is generated by npm run build in /brand.

01The mark

A service cloche with ring arcs: the order is ready, and the phone rings. It is built on a 200-unit grid. One unit does all the spacing: K, the diameter of the handle (18 grid units). The mark is exactly 7K tall.

  • Dome carries the colour. Its gloss is a true knock-out, so the mark stays correct on any ground.
  • Rim and plate line share the fired clay in the colour version; the plate line drops out below 64 px.
  • Ring arcs: two per side at full size, one per side at M, none at S.
  • Tile corner radius is 22% of the edge (44 / 200).

Mark-only files (no tile) are cropped tight to the artwork, ready to place with the clear space below.

02Clear space and spacing

Keep 2K clear on every side of any logo. Inside the lockups everything is set from the same unit, so scaling a lockup never changes its proportions. Never rebuild one by eye.

Clear space = 2K on all sides, measured from the artwork edge. On a tile or a photo, the tile or photo edge counts as the artwork edge.

The wordmark sits on the rim's lower edge (shared baseline). Mark to word gap 2K (stacked: 1.5K). Bilingual pair: 1.5K either side of a 4-unit divider.

03Sizes and optical versions

Detail that works at 200 px turns to mud at 24 px, so the mark comes in three optical sizes. Pick by rendered size, never by file.

L at 96px

Full: ring arcs, gloss, plate line, handle stem. Tile 64 px and up.

M at 48px

Reduced: one heavier arc per side, no gloss or stem. Tile 40 to 63 px.

S at 32px and 16px

Minimal: dome, handle, rim. Tile 16 to 39 px, favicon.

05Colourways

Six colourways, all transparent SVG. Colour on slip white or clay slip, On dark on fired clay or ink, Reverse on red ochre, and a one-colour version when you only have one ink.

06Typography

The wordmark is Alexandria Bold, lowercase, default kerning, converted to outlines so no font is needed to use it. Latin and Arabic come from one family, which is why the pair sits so evenly. Arabic is written رنة without vowel marks.

07Lockup library

Nine arrangements. Horizontal for headers and bars, stacked for squares and signage, Arabic and bilingual versions for RTL surfaces. In the Arabic horizontal lockup the mark sits on the right, where an Arabic reader starts.

Tiles

08App icons and social

The ochre tile is the app icon. Rounded where we draw the corner (favicon, 192, 512), full-bleed where the OS rounds it (Apple touch icon, maskable). The maskable mark is held inside the 80% safe circle. Nothing here replaces public/icons until you copy it across.

09Don't

If you need something these files don't cover, add it to the generator instead of editing a copy.
